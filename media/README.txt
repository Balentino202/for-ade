Put her videos, photos and your song in this folder.

  our-song.mp3   -> background music        (set in src/data.ts -> music)
  video-1.mp4    -> first "Moments" video   (set in src/data.ts -> moments)
  video-2.mp4, photo-1.jpg, ... -> more moments

Tips
- Use .mp4 (H.264) for videos so every phone can play them. iPhone .mov
  files: export/share as "Most Compatible" or convert to .mp4.
- Keep each video short (5-20 seconds) and under ~20 MB so the site
  loads fast once it is online.
- File names are case-sensitive once deployed: video-1.mp4 is not Video-1.MP4.
